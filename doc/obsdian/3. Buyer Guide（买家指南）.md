# English

## Introduction

HeatEx Direct is an independent industrial sourcing platform designed to help global buyers identify and connect with verified Chinese heat exchanger manufacturers. The platform focuses specifically on industrial heat exchange equipment such as plate heat exchangers, shell and tube heat exchangers, air coolers, and other thermal transfer solutions used in industries including HVAC, petrochemical, marine, energy, and food processing.

This guide explains how buyers can use the platform to find reliable manufacturers, submit RFQ requests, and evaluate supplier proposals efficiently. Our goal is to simplify international sourcing while protecting buyer privacy and ensuring that manufacturers on the platform are real production facilities.

---

## Who This Platform Is For

HeatEx Direct is designed primarily for engineers, procurement managers, project developers, and industrial equipment buyers who are searching for reliable heat exchanger manufacturers in China.

Typical users of the platform include EPC contractors, equipment distributors, plant engineers, project procurement teams, and industrial system integrators. These professionals often need to identify suppliers that meet strict technical requirements while maintaining compliance with international standards such as ASME, ISO, or PED.

The platform helps buyers quickly identify capable manufacturers without spending weeks verifying company credentials.

---

## How the Platform Works

HeatEx Direct simplifies the sourcing process through a structured workflow.

Buyers can browse verified manufacturer profiles to understand each company's capabilities, certifications, production capacity, and export experience. When buyers have a specific project requirement, they can submit a Request for Quotation through the platform's standardized RFQ system.

Once an RFQ is submitted, the system identifies manufacturers that match the technical requirements. These manufacturers can review the request and provide technical proposals or quotations.

Buyers can then compare different supplier responses and choose which companies they want to contact directly for further discussion.

---

## How to Submit an RFQ

Submitting a Request for Quotation on HeatEx Direct is designed to be simple and structured.

Buyers begin by entering the technical specifications of their project through a guided form. This includes parameters such as heat exchanger type, operating temperature range, pressure conditions, fluid types, materials, and industry application.

Providing clear technical information helps ensure that manufacturers who receive the RFQ are capable of delivering the required solution.

After completing the form, buyers can choose whether to submit the RFQ anonymously or reveal their contact information immediately.

Once submitted, the RFQ will be distributed to verified manufacturers whose capabilities match the project requirements.

---

## Anonymous RFQ Mode

Many procurement professionals prefer not to expose their contact information at the early stage of supplier discovery. For this reason, HeatEx Direct offers a Stealth RFQ mode.

In Stealth mode, buyers can submit detailed technical requirements without revealing their identity. Manufacturers will see the project specifications but will not have access to the buyer's contact information.

This allows buyers to review proposals and compare supplier capabilities without receiving unsolicited sales emails or calls. When the buyer decides to move forward with a specific manufacturer, they can then choose to reveal their contact information.

---

## What Happens After Submission

After an RFQ is submitted, the platform matches the request with manufacturers that have relevant production capabilities.

Manufacturers who receive the RFQ may respond with technical proposals, clarifying questions, or preliminary quotations. Buyers can review these responses and evaluate suppliers based on technical capability, experience, and proposed solutions.

If the buyer wishes to continue discussions, they can establish direct communication with the manufacturer to finalize specifications, pricing, and delivery terms.

HeatEx Direct does not act as an intermediary in the transaction. The platform's role is to facilitate the initial connection between buyers and verified manufacturers.

---

## Supplier Verification

A key feature of HeatEx Direct is the verification process applied to manufacturers before they are listed on the platform.

Our analysts review official business licenses to confirm that the company is registered as a manufacturing enterprise. We also examine company information, production capabilities, and relevant certifications such as ASME or ISO when applicable.

In addition, export records and facility locations may be reviewed to confirm that the company has real production capacity and international project experience.

This verification process helps reduce the risk of dealing with trading companies or intermediaries.

---

## Best Practices for Buyers

To obtain the best results when using the platform, buyers should provide as much technical detail as possible in their RFQ submissions.

Clear engineering specifications allow manufacturers to evaluate whether they can meet the project requirements. This reduces unnecessary communication and helps ensure that only qualified suppliers respond to the request.

Buyers are also encouraged to review manufacturer profiles carefully and consider factors such as certification status, export experience, and technical specialization when evaluating potential partners.

---

## Cost and Fees

HeatEx Direct is free for buyers to use.

Buyers can browse manufacturer profiles, access verified supplier information, and submit RFQ requests without paying any platform fees.

Our business model is based on partnerships with manufacturers rather than charging buyers for access. This ensures that procurement teams can use the platform freely while maintaining transparency in the sourcing process.

---

## Our Mission

HeatEx Direct aims to become the most trusted platform for sourcing industrial heat exchangers from China.

By combining manual supplier verification, structured sourcing tools, and transparent data presentation, we help engineers and procurement teams make better sourcing decisions with confidence.

Our mission is to bridge the trust gap between global buyers and Chinese manufacturers while enabling efficient and reliable industrial sourcing.

# 中文版本

## 平台简介

HeatEx Direct 是一个独立的工业采购平台，旨在帮助全球采购方找到并联系经过验证的中国换热器制造企业。平台专注于工业换热设备领域，包括板式换热器、管壳式换热器、空气冷却器以及其他用于 HVAC、石化、海洋工程、能源和食品加工等行业的换热设备。

本指南将介绍采购方如何使用平台寻找制造商、提交询价请求以及评估供应商方案。我们的目标是在保护采购方隐私的同时，让国际工业采购变得更加简单和可靠。

---

## 平台适用对象

HeatEx Direct 主要面向工程师、采购经理、项目开发人员以及工业设备采购人员，这些用户通常需要寻找可靠的中国换热器制造商。

平台的典型用户包括 EPC 工程公司、设备经销商、工厂工程师、项目采购团队以及工业系统集成商。这些专业人士往往需要寻找能够满足严格技术要求并符合国际标准（如 ASME、ISO 或 PED）的设备供应商。

HeatEx Direct 可以帮助这些用户在更短时间内识别可靠制造商，而无需花费大量时间进行企业背景调查。

---

## 平台如何运作

HeatEx Direct 通过结构化流程简化工业设备采购。

采购方可以浏览平台上的制造企业资料，了解企业的技术能力、认证情况、生产规模以及出口经验。当采购方有具体项目需求时，可以通过平台的标准化 RFQ 系统提交询价请求。

系统会根据技术需求匹配合适的制造企业，这些企业可以查看项目需求并提交技术方案或报价。

采购方可以比较不同供应商的方案，并选择希望进一步沟通的企业。

---

## 如何提交询价

在 HeatEx Direct 提交询价非常简单。

采购方需要通过表单填写项目的关键技术参数，例如换热器类型、工作温度范围、压力条件、流体类型、材料要求以及应用行业。

提供清晰的技术信息可以帮助制造企业判断是否具备相应能力，从而提高匹配效率。

完成表单后，采购方可以选择匿名提交 RFQ，或直接提供联系方式。

提交之后，系统会将询价信息发送给符合技术要求的已验证制造企业。

---

## 匿名询价模式

许多采购专业人士在供应商筛选的早期阶段并不希望公开联系方式。为此，HeatEx Direct 提供匿名询价模式。

在该模式下，采购方可以提交详细的技术需求，而无需公开身份信息。制造企业可以查看项目需求，但无法看到采购方的联系方式。

这样采购方可以在不被大量销售邮件打扰的情况下，对不同供应商的方案进行评估。当采购方决定与某个制造企业进一步沟通时，可以再选择公开联系方式。

---

## 提交后会发生什么

当 RFQ 提交后，系统会根据技术能力匹配合适的制造企业。

制造企业可能会提交技术方案、提出问题或提供初步报价。采购方可以根据技术能力、项目经验以及解决方案质量来评估这些企业。

如果采购方希望继续推进项目，可以直接与制造企业建立联系，并进一步讨论技术细节、价格以及交付条件。

HeatEx Direct 并不会作为交易中介参与交易。平台的作用是帮助采购方与已验证制造企业建立初步联系。

---

## 供应商验证机制

HeatEx Direct 的一个重要特点是对制造企业进行验证。

平台分析人员会检查企业营业执照，以确认该公司属于制造企业。同时还会审核企业信息、生产能力以及相关认证，例如 ASME 或 ISO。

此外，平台还可能通过出口记录和工厂地址验证来确认企业具有真实生产能力和国际项目经验。

这一验证机制可以降低采购方遇到贸易公司或中间商的风险。
