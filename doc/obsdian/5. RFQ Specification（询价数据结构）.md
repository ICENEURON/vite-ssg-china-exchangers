# English

## Introduction

The Request for Quotation (RFQ) specification defines the structured data required for buyers to request quotations from verified manufacturers on the HeatEx Direct platform.  

The purpose of this structure is to standardize technical information so that manufacturers can quickly evaluate project requirements and determine whether they are capable of delivering the requested equipment.

A well-structured RFQ significantly improves sourcing efficiency by reducing unnecessary communication and ensuring that only technically suitable manufacturers receive the request.

The RFQ system is designed specifically for industrial heat exchanger projects and focuses on collecting the most relevant engineering parameters.

---

## RFQ Data Structure Overview

An RFQ record typically consists of several logical sections:

Project Information  
Buyer Information  
Technical Specifications  
Operating Conditions  
Design Requirements  
Commercial Requirements  
Attachments and Supporting Documents

Each section contains structured fields that allow the platform to store, process, and distribute RFQ requests efficiently.

---

## Project Information

This section captures general information about the project and helps manufacturers understand the application context.

Typical fields include:

Project Title  
Project Industry  
Project Country  
Project Location  
Project Description  
Estimated Project Timeline

This information helps suppliers understand the environment in which the heat exchanger will operate and the expected project schedule.

---

## Buyer Information

Buyer information allows manufacturers to contact the buyer when the buyer chooses to reveal their identity.

Typical fields include:

Company Name  
Contact Person  
Email Address  
Phone Number  
Country  
Preferred Communication Method

If the buyer selects Stealth RFQ mode, these fields remain hidden from manufacturers until the buyer decides to reveal them.

---

## Equipment Type

This section defines the general category of heat exchanger being requested.

Typical fields include:

Heat Exchanger Type  
Plate Heat Exchanger  
Shell and Tube Heat Exchanger  
Air Cooled Heat Exchanger  
Plate and Shell Heat Exchanger  
Custom Heat Exchanger

Selecting the equipment type allows the platform to match the RFQ with manufacturers specializing in that product category.

---

## Process Fluids

Heat exchanger design depends heavily on the fluids involved in the process.

Typical fields include:

Hot Side Fluid  
Cold Side Fluid  
Fluid Phase (Liquid / Gas / Two Phase)  
Fluid Composition (if known)

Providing accurate fluid information helps manufacturers estimate fouling factors and material compatibility.

---

## Operating Conditions

Operating conditions define the thermal and pressure parameters required for the heat exchanger.

Typical fields include:

Hot Side Inlet Temperature  
Hot Side Outlet Temperature  
Cold Side Inlet Temperature  
Cold Side Outlet Temperature  
Operating Pressure  
Design Pressure  
Flow Rate

These parameters are critical for heat transfer calculations and equipment sizing.

---

## Materials and Construction

This section describes material requirements for the equipment.

Typical fields include:

Material of Construction  
Carbon Steel  
Stainless Steel  
Duplex Stainless Steel  
Titanium  
Other Special Materials

Additional fields may include corrosion considerations or coating requirements.

---

## Design Requirements

This section captures engineering standards and certification requirements.

Typical fields include:

Design Standard (ASME / TEMA / GB / Other)  
Required Certifications  
ASME U Stamp  
PED Compliance  
CE Marking

These fields ensure that manufacturers proposing solutions can comply with the necessary regulatory standards.

---

## Installation and Application

This section provides additional context about how the equipment will be used.

Typical fields include:

Application Industry  
HVAC  
Petrochemical  
Marine  
Power Generation  
Food Processing

Additional optional fields may include installation environment and space limitations.

---

## Commercial Requirements

This section includes commercial information relevant to the quotation process.

Typical fields include:

Estimated Quantity  
Required Delivery Time  
Target Delivery Location  
Budget Range (optional)

Although optional, this information helps manufacturers prepare realistic quotations.

---

## Attachments and Supporting Documents

Buyers may upload additional files to clarify technical requirements.

Typical attachments include:

Process Flow Diagrams  
Engineering Drawings  
Technical Specifications  
Existing Equipment Data Sheets

Providing supporting documentation can significantly improve quotation accuracy.

---

## RFQ Workflow

The RFQ workflow typically follows these steps.

First, the buyer submits the RFQ form by entering structured technical data.  
Second, the platform stores the RFQ and automatically identifies manufacturers that match the required capabilities.  
Third, matching manufacturers receive the RFQ notification and review the project requirements.  
Fourth, manufacturers respond with technical proposals or quotations.

Buyers can then review and compare responses before initiating direct communication with selected manufacturers.

---

## Data Usage

RFQ data supports multiple functions within the platform.

It allows automatic supplier matching based on technical capabilities.  
It enables structured comparison of quotations.  
It also contributes to aggregated analytics that help improve sourcing recommendations over time.

All RFQ information is handled with strict privacy protection, especially when the buyer selects anonymous submission.

# 中文版本

## 概述

RFQ（询价请求）数据结构用于定义采购方在 HeatEx Direct 平台上提交询价时所需要提供的标准化信息。

该结构的主要目标是将技术需求标准化，从而使制造企业能够快速理解项目需求并判断是否具备生产能力。

一个结构化的 RFQ 能够显著提升采购效率，因为它减少了不必要的沟通，并确保只有技术能力匹配的企业才会收到询价请求。

该 RFQ 系统专门针对工业换热设备项目设计，重点收集最关键的工程参数。

---

## RFQ 数据结构概览

一个 RFQ 通常包含以下几个主要模块：

项目信息  
采购方信息  
设备技术参数  
运行条件  
设计要求  
商业信息  
附件与技术文件

这些模块中的字段会被平台存储并用于匹配合适的制造企业。

---

## 项目信息

该模块用于描述项目的基本背景信息。

常见字段包括：

项目名称  
项目行业  
项目所在国家  
项目地点  
项目描述  
预计项目时间

这些信息可以帮助制造企业理解设备的应用环境以及项目进度。

---

## 采购方信息

采购方信息用于在采购方允许的情况下与制造企业建立联系。

常见字段包括：

公司名称  
联系人姓名  
邮箱地址  
联系电话  
国家  
首选沟通方式

如果采购方启用了匿名 RFQ 模式，这些信息在初期不会向制造企业展示。

---

## 设备类型

该模块用于定义所需换热设备的基本类型。

常见选项包括：

板式换热器  
管壳式换热器  
空气冷却器  
板壳式换热器  
定制换热设备

通过设备类型，平台可以匹配相关制造企业。

---

## 工艺流体

换热器设计高度依赖工艺流体信息。

常见字段包括：

热侧流体  
冷侧流体  
流体状态（液体 / 气体 / 两相）  
流体组成（如已知）

准确的流体信息有助于制造企业评估结垢风险以及材料兼容性。

---

## 运行条件

运行条件定义了换热器所需满足的温度和压力条件。

常见字段包括：

热侧入口温度  
热侧出口温度  
冷侧入口温度  
冷侧出口温度  
工作压力  
设计压力  
流量

这些参数对于换热计算和设备尺寸设计至关重要。

---

## 材料要求

该模块描述设备所需材料。

常见字段包括：

设备材料  
碳钢  
不锈钢  
双相钢  
钛  
其他特殊材料

也可以包括防腐要求或表面处理要求。

---

## 设计标准

该模块记录设计标准和认证要求。

常见字段包括：

设计标准（ASME / TEMA / GB 等）  
认证要求  
ASME U Stamp  
PED 认证  
CE 标志

这些信息可以确保制造企业提供符合相关法规的设备。

---

## 安装与应用

该模块提供设备使用场景。

常见字段包括：

应用行业  
HVAC  
石化  
海洋工程  
电力  
食品加工

还可以包含安装环境或空间限制等信息。

---

## 商业信息

该模块包含与报价相关的商业信息。

常见字段包括：

需求数量  
期望交付时间  
交付地点  
预算范围（可选）

这些信息能够帮助制造企业提供更准确的报价。

---

## 附件与技术文件

采购方可以上传技术文件来补充说明需求。

常见附件包括：

工艺流程图  
工程图纸  
技术规格书  
现有设备数据表

这些资料可以显著提升报价准确度。

---

## RFQ 工作流程

RFQ 通常按照以下流程进行。

采购方首先填写并提交 RFQ 表单。  
平台随后根据技术能力自动匹配制造企业。  
匹配企业收到通知并查看项目需求。  
制造企业提交技术方案或报价。

采购方可以比较不同企业的回复，并选择希望进一步沟通的供应商。

---

## 数据用途

RFQ 数据在平台中具有多种用途。

它可以支持自动供应商匹配、报价比较以及采购分析。同时，这些数据还可以帮助平台不断优化供应商推荐算法。

所有 RFQ 信息都会受到严格保护，尤其是在匿名询价模式下，采购方身份信息不会被公开。
