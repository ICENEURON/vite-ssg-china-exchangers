# English

## Introduction

The Supplier Database is the core infrastructure of the HeatEx Direct platform. It stores structured information about verified heat exchanger manufacturers and enables buyers to discover suppliers based on technical capabilities, certifications, and industry applications.

The purpose of this database is not only to store company information, but also to standardize supplier data in a way that allows accurate filtering, comparison, and RFQ matching.

Each supplier profile represents a real manufacturing entity and contains structured fields that describe the company’s identity, production capabilities, certifications, export experience, and product specialization.

---

## Supplier Profile Overview

Each manufacturer in the database has a unique supplier profile. The supplier profile serves as the primary reference point for buyers and for internal verification processes.

A supplier profile typically includes the following major categories:

Company Identity  
Manufacturing Capability  
Certifications and Compliance  
Product Portfolio  
Export Experience  
Factory Information  
Media and Documentation

This structured design allows engineers and procurement teams to quickly understand whether a manufacturer is suitable for a specific project.

---

## Company Identity

This section stores basic company information that identifies the manufacturer.

Typical fields include:

Company Name (English)  
Company Name (Chinese)  
Official Website  
Registered Address  
Factory Location  
Year Founded  
Number of Employees  
Ownership Type (Private / State-owned / Joint Venture)

This information allows buyers to understand the company background and scale.

---

## Manufacturing Capability

Manufacturing capability fields describe the technical and production capacity of the factory.

Typical fields include:

Manufacturing Scope  
Primary Products  
Maximum Design Pressure  
Maximum Operating Temperature  
Heat Exchanger Types Supported  
Materials Supported (e.g. Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Titanium)  
Production Capacity (Units per Year)  
Engineering Design Capability (Yes / No)

These fields allow the platform to match RFQs with manufacturers that can technically deliver the required equipment.

---

## Certifications and Compliance

Industrial equipment procurement often requires strict certification compliance. This section records all relevant certifications held by the manufacturer.

Typical fields include:

ASME Certification  
ISO 9001  
ISO 14001  
ISO 45001  
PED Compliance  
CE Marking  
Other Industry Certifications

Each certification may include supporting information such as certificate number, issuing authority, and expiration date.

---

## Product Portfolio

This section describes the types of heat exchanger products manufactured by the supplier.

Typical product categories include:

Plate Heat Exchangers  
Shell and Tube Heat Exchangers  
Air Coolers  
Plate and Shell Heat Exchangers  
Welded Plate Heat Exchangers  
Custom Heat Exchangers

Each product category can include additional attributes such as design standards, application industries, and typical project sizes.

---

## Export Experience

Export experience helps buyers evaluate whether a manufacturer has experience serving international markets.

Typical fields include:

Primary Export Markets  
Major Project Regions  
Years of Export Experience  
Key International Clients (if public)  
Shipping History Indicators

Export experience data helps confirm that the manufacturer is capable of working with international procurement processes.

---

## Factory Information

Factory information provides insight into the actual production environment.

Typical fields include:

Factory Area  
Number of Production Lines  
Workshop Facilities  
Major Manufacturing Equipment  
Quality Control Procedures  
Testing Capabilities

These fields allow buyers to better understand the production capacity and engineering environment of the manufacturer.

---

## Media and Documentation

This section stores supporting media and documents that help buyers evaluate suppliers visually and technically.

Typical items include:

Factory Photos  
Workshop Photos  
Product Images  
Certification Documents  
Technical Brochures

These assets improve transparency and help buyers gain confidence in the supplier’s capabilities.

---

## Verification Status

Each supplier profile includes a verification status field indicating the level of validation performed by the platform.

Possible statuses may include:

Pending Verification  
Verified Manufacturer  
Contributor Manufacturer  
Flagged for Review

Verification status allows buyers to quickly understand the reliability level of the supplier profile.

---

## Data Usage

The supplier database serves multiple purposes within the platform.

It enables buyers to search and filter manufacturers based on technical capabilities, certifications, and industries served. It also supports the RFQ matching system by identifying manufacturers capable of meeting specific technical requirements.

Additionally, the database forms the foundation for analytics, supplier ranking, and content publishing across the platform.

---

## Long Term Database Vision

Over time, the HeatEx Direct supplier database is intended to become one of the most reliable structured datasets for Chinese heat exchanger manufacturers.

By maintaining strict verification standards and continuously updating company data, the platform aims to build a transparent and trustworthy ecosystem where buyers can confidently identify capable industrial manufacturers.

# 中文版本

## 概述

供应商数据库是 HeatEx Direct 平台的核心基础设施。该数据库用于存储经过验证的换热器制造企业信息，并帮助采购方根据技术能力、认证情况以及行业应用快速找到合适的供应商。

数据库不仅仅用于存储企业资料，更重要的是通过结构化数据的方式，使供应商信息可以被有效筛选、比较以及用于 RFQ 匹配。

每一个供应商资料代表一个真实的制造企业，并包含企业身份信息、生产能力、认证情况、出口经验以及产品类型等结构化字段。

---

## 供应商资料结构

数据库中的每个制造企业都拥有一个独立的供应商资料页面。该资料既用于采购方浏览，也用于平台内部的验证管理。

一个完整的供应商资料通常包含以下几个主要模块：

企业身份信息
制造能力
认证与合规信息
产品组合
出口经验  
工厂信息  
媒体与文档

这种结构化设计能够帮助工程师和采购团队快速判断企业是否适合某个项目需求。

---

## 企业身份信息

该模块用于存储企业的基本信息，用于识别制造企业身份。

常见字段包括：

企业英文名称  
企业中文名称  
官方网站
注册地址
工厂地址
成立年份
员工数量
企业类型（民营 / 国有 / 合资）

这些信息可以帮助采购方了解企业规模与背景。

---

## 制造能力

制造能力字段用于描述企业的工程能力和生产能力。

常见字段包括：

制造范围
主要产品
最大设计压力
最大工作温度
支持的换热器类型
支持的材料（如碳钢、不锈钢、钛）
年生产能力
是否具备工程设计能力

这些数据可以帮助平台将 RFQ 项目匹配到具备相应技术能力的制造企业。

## 认证与合规信息

ISO 9001

板式换热器
