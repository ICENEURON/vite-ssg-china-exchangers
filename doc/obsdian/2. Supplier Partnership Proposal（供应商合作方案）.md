# English

## Introduction

HeatEx Direct is an independent industrial sourcing platform focused on connecting global buyers with verified Chinese heat exchanger manufacturers. The platform specializes in industrial heat exchange equipment, including plate heat exchangers, shell and tube heat exchangers, air coolers, and other thermal transfer solutions used in industries such as HVAC, petrochemical, marine, energy, and food processing.

Our mission is to help international buyers identify reliable Chinese manufacturers more efficiently while helping capable factories gain global visibility. Instead of competing on large marketplaces with thousands of suppliers, HeatEx Direct provides a curated and technically verified environment designed specifically for engineering driven procurement.

We invite qualified manufacturers to participate in the platform and present their engineering capabilities to a global audience of procurement professionals.

Every month, engineers, procurement managers, and project developers from around the world search for industrial heat exchanger suppliers. HeatEx Direct helps manufacturers reach these buyers through a focused platform designed specifically for technical sourcing.

Unlike large marketplaces where suppliers compete with thousands of listings, our platform emphasizes quality over quantity. This allows manufacturers with real production capability to stand out.

---

### Increase International Visibility

Many highly capable Chinese manufacturers have strong engineering teams and production facilities but limited global marketing exposure. HeatEx Direct helps bridge this gap by presenting verified manufacturer profiles to an international audience.

Through our platform, your company can gain visibility among global buyers who are actively searching for reliable industrial equipment suppliers.

Modern procurement decisions are driven by technical trust. Buyers want to understand not only what a company sells, but also what it can engineer and deliver.

Manufacturers on HeatEx Direct can publish technical articles, engineering case studies, and application guides. These publications help demonstrate engineering expertise while establishing your company as a trusted technical authority in the industry.

## How the Platform Works

### Step 1: Manufacturer Identification

Our team identifies potential manufacturers through industry databases, export records, certifications, and public company information. If your company has been listed in our database, it means we have identified your organization as a potential manufacturer in the heat exchanger industry.

Manufacturers can claim and update their profile to ensure the information accurately represents their current capabilities.

---

### Step 2: Verification Process

To maintain a high level of trust, every company profile on HeatEx Direct undergoes a verification process. This includes reviewing official business licenses to confirm manufacturing activities, validating certifications such as ASME and ISO when applicable, and confirming factory locations.

This verification process ensures that buyers interacting with suppliers on the platform are engaging with real manufacturers rather than intermediaries.

---

### Step 3: Profile Activation

Once verification is completed, the manufacturer profile is activated on the platform. The profile can include company background, engineering capabilities, production capacity, certifications, export markets, and product specializations.

Manufacturers may also include factory photos, workshop images, and product visuals to better represent their manufacturing environment.

---

### Step 4: Buyer Discovery and RFQ Requests

Global buyers browsing the platform can discover verified manufacturers and evaluate their technical capabilities. Buyers can submit RFQ requests through the platform's structured specification system.

Manufacturers that match the technical requirements may receive relevant RFQ opportunities and can respond directly with proposals and quotations.

---

## Participation Requirements

To maintain a high quality sourcing environment, HeatEx Direct requires participating manufacturers to meet several criteria.

Companies must be real manufacturing organizations rather than trading intermediaries. Valid business licenses must confirm that manufacturing activities are included in the registered business scope.

Manufacturers should provide accurate company information including production capabilities, key certifications, and technical specialization. Supporting documentation such as business licenses and certificates may be required during verification.

The platform reserves the right to reject or remove profiles that cannot provide sufficient verification evidence.

我们的使命是帮助国际采购方更高效地找到可靠的中国制造企业，同时也帮助具备工程能力的工厂获得更多国际曝光。与拥有成千上万供应商的大型综合平台不同，HeatEx Direct 提供的是一个经过筛选和技术验证的环境，更适合工程型采购需求。

我们诚邀符合条件的制造企业加入平台，并向全球采购团队展示自身的工程能力。

## 平台运作方式

### 第一步：制造商识别

平台通过行业数据库、出口记录、认证信息以及公开企业资料识别潜在制造企业。如果您的公司已经出现在平台数据库中，说明我们已经将贵公司识别为换热器行业的潜在制造商。

企业可以申请认领并更新企业资料，以确保平台信息准确反映企业的真实能力。

---

### 第二步：企业验证

为了确保平台的可信度，所有企业资料都需要经过验证流程。验证内容包括营业执照检查、经营范围确认、ASME 和 ISO 等认证核验以及工厂地址确认。

这一验证流程能够确保采购方在平台上接触到的是实际制造企业，而不是贸易中介。

---

### 第三步：企业资料上线

完成验证后，企业资料将在平台上正式上线。资料页面可以包含公司介绍、工程能力、生产规模、认证证书、出口市场以及产品类型等信息。

企业还可以上传工厂照片、生产车间照片以及产品图片，以更全面地展示企业实力。

---

### 第四步：采购方发现与询价

全球采购方可以浏览平台上的企业资料，并根据技术能力筛选供应商。采购方还可以通过平台的标准化询价系统提交 RFQ 技术需求。

符合技术条件的制造企业可能会收到相关询价机会，并可以直接向采购方提交技术方案和报价。
