# Supplier Partnership Proposal / 供应商合作方案

Last updated: 2026-05-08.

## English

HeatEx Direct gives qualified Chinese heat exchanger manufacturers a focused international profile channel. The platform is designed for engineering-driven buyers who care about production capability, technical documentation, applications, certifications, and export experience.

### Why Manufacturers Should Participate

- Reach buyers who are specifically searching for heat exchanger suppliers.
- Present company and product capability outside of noisy general marketplaces.
- Build credibility through structured profile data, factory media, certificates, and technical content.
- Improve long-term search visibility through manufacturer profiles, product pages, and technical articles.
- Receive qualified RFQ opportunities as the platform's RFQ operations mature.

### Current Platform Offer

- Manufacturer profile page.
- Product listing and product detail pages.
- Company images, certificates, customer logos, documents, and product media.
- Supplier claim page for profile correction or ownership requests.
- Content marketing page for technical article and case-study submissions.
- Contact route through `contact@heatexdirect.com`.

### Manufacturer Data Needed

- Legal company name in English and Chinese.
- Short and full company descriptions.
- Website, email, phone, city, address, country, founding year, factory area, and employee count.
- Industries served and export markets.
- Product list with descriptions, advantages, technical parameters, details, and images.
- Certificates, factory photos, customer logos, documents, and optional videos.
- SEO metadata where available.

### Verification Expectations

The platform should prioritize real manufacturers. Verification materials may include business licenses, certificate scans, factory addresses, public registry checks, official website checks, export records, and supporting product documentation.

### Commercial Model

The buyer side should remain free. Supplier-side monetization can include profile enhancement, sponsored technical content, premium visibility, and referral or service fees when genuine business opportunities are created.

### Operational Honesty

Current RFQ code saves verified buyer requests into Supabase. Automatic matching and supplier notification are not implemented yet. Supplier materials should describe RFQ distribution as an operational or future capability until the code and internal process exist.

## 中文

HeatEx Direct 为合格的中国换热器制造商提供一个聚焦国际采购的展示渠道。平台面向重视生产能力、技术资料、应用行业、认证证书和出口经验的工程型买家。

### 制造商为什么要参与

- 接触正在寻找换热器供应商的精准买家。
- 在大型综合平台之外展示真实工厂和产品能力。
- 通过结构化资料、工厂图片、证书和技术内容建立可信度。
- 通过厂家页面、产品页面和技术文章提升长期搜索曝光。
- 随着 RFQ 运营成熟，获得更高质量的询价机会。

### 当前平台可提供

- 制造商资料页。
- 产品列表和产品详情页。
- 公司图片、证书、客户 Logo、文档和产品媒体。
- 厂家资料认领页面，用于资料更正或所有权申请。
- 内容营销页面，用于提交技术文章和案例研究。
- 通过 `contact@heatexdirect.com` 建立联系。

### 需要厂家提供的数据

- 中英文公司法定名称。
- 公司短介绍和完整介绍。
- 官网、邮箱、电话、城市、地址、国家、成立年份、厂房面积、员工数量。
- 服务行业和出口市场。
- 产品列表、产品描述、优势、技术参数、详情和图片。
- 证书、工厂照片、客户 Logo、文档和可选视频。
- 可用的 SEO 信息。

### 验证要求

平台应优先展示真实制造企业。验证材料可以包括营业执照、证书扫描件、工厂地址、公开注册信息、官网信息、出口记录和产品资料。

### 商业模式

采购方应保持免费。供应商侧可探索资料增强展示、技术内容推广、推荐展示，以及真实商机产生后的推荐或服务费用。

### 运营边界

当前 RFQ 代码会将已验证邮箱的买家需求写入 Supabase。自动匹配和供应商通知尚未实现。供应商合作材料应把 RFQ 分发描述为运营能力或未来能力，直到代码和内部流程都已经落地。
