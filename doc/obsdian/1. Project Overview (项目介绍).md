# Project Overview / 项目介绍

Last updated: 2026-05-08.

## English

HeatEx Direct is an independent information service focused on verified Chinese heat exchanger manufacturers. It is built for global buyers who need to identify real factories, compare technical capabilities, and submit structured quote requests for industrial heat exchange equipment.

The company should be described as an independent information service with Australia-based operations. It is not limited to the Australian market; its buyer audience is global. The current team combines three capabilities: senior first-hand knowledge of the Chinese heat exchanger industry, international marketing, and software/data engineering.

The platform covers plate heat exchangers, welded plate heat exchangers, shell-and-tube equipment, air-side heat exchange equipment, spare parts, and related thermal transfer solutions used in HVAC, petrochemical, marine, energy, food processing, pharmaceutical, and power generation applications.

The current website is a static-first React/Vite SSG application. Manufacturer and product pages are generated from Supabase data that is synced into local JSON, while quote request submission and email verification use Supabase live services.

### Current Product Functions

- Verified manufacturer directory.
- Product catalogue and product detail pages.
- Bilingual English/Chinese user interface.
- Industry news section powered by Markdown and Velite when blog is enabled.
- Smart quote request form with project context, thermal/design parameters, email OTP verification, and Supabase insertion.
- Supplier claim and content marketing pages.
- Optional login/register/dashboard routes when auth is enabled.

### Important Current Limitation

Quote requests are currently saved to the `rfqs` table. The code does not yet automatically match requests to suppliers, email manufacturers, or populate `manufacturers_sent_list`. Any public or sales copy should describe routing as a future or manual operational workflow until it is implemented.

### Platform Principle

HeatEx Direct should compete on trust, structure, and technical clarity rather than supplier volume. The goal is not to list every possible company, but to make verified manufacturer data easier for serious buyers to inspect.

Industry News should become the main traffic acquisition channel. Articles should focus on company news, technical articles, supplier verification, product explainers, quote request preparation, China market intelligence, and application-specific sourcing advice.

## 中文

HeatEx Direct 是一项专注于中国换热器制造商的独立工业采购研究服务。它面向全球采购方，帮助他们识别真实工厂、比较技术能力，并提交结构化的换热设备询价需求。

公司应描述为一项具有澳洲运营背景的独立信息服务，服务对象不只是澳洲本地市场，而是全球工业采购方。当前团队由三类能力组成：非常熟悉中国换热器行业真实情况的资深行业经验人员、国际marketing推广能力，以及网站/数据自动化工程能力。

平台覆盖板式换热器、焊接板式换热器、管壳式换热设备、空气侧换热设备、备件以及其他工业换热解决方案，应用行业包括 HVAC、石化、海洋工程、能源、食品加工、制药和电力等。

当前网站采用静态优先架构：厂家和产品页面由 Supabase 数据同步生成的本地 JSON 进行静态渲染；询价提交和邮箱验证则使用 Supabase 的实时服务。

### 当前功能

- 已验证制造商目录。
- 产品列表和产品详情页。
- 英文/繁体中文双语界面。
- 智能询价表单，包含项目背景、热工/设计参数、邮箱 OTP 验证和 Supabase 入库。
- 厂家资料认领页面和内容营销服务页面。
- 基于 Markdown 和 Velite 的行业资讯模块，在 `VITE_ENABLE_BLOG=true` 时启用。

### 项目原则

HeatEx Direct 的核心竞争力应该是信任、结构化数据和技术清晰度，而不是供应商数量。目标不是收录所有公司，而是让严肃采购方更容易检查可信制造商。

Industry News 应作为主要引流窗口。文章方向应聚焦买家指南、供应商核验、产品解释、RFQ 准备、中国市场信息，以及具体应用场景下的采购建议。
