# English

## Introduction

HeatEx Direct is an independent industrial sourcing platform designed to connect global buyers directly with verified Chinese heat exchanger manufacturers. The platform focuses specifically on the heat exchanger industry, including plate heat exchangers, shell and tube heat exchangers, air coolers, and other industrial thermal exchange equipment.

Our goal is to simplify international sourcing while increasing transparency and trust between buyers and manufacturers. Instead of acting as an intermediary marketplace, HeatEx Direct functions as a verification and sourcing bridge that allows engineers and procurement teams to identify reliable manufacturers more efficiently.

---

## Industry Challenges

Global buyers sourcing industrial heat exchangers often face several challenges when working with overseas suppliers.

Most online directories contain thousands of suppliers, but very few of them are actually verified. Many listings represent trading companies rather than real factories, which makes it difficult to determine who actually manufactures the equipment.

Technical information on many supplier websites is incomplete or outdated. Procurement teams often spend weeks exchanging emails, requesting documentation, and verifying certifications before they can confidently shortlist potential suppliers.

This lack of transparency creates significant friction in international sourcing, especially for engineering-driven industries where reliability, compliance, and technical capability are critical.

---

## Our Approach

HeatEx Direct was created to address this trust gap.

Instead of building a massive directory of thousands of suppliers, the platform focuses on identifying a smaller number of capable manufacturers with proven engineering capabilities. Each company listed on the platform undergoes a structured verification process before appearing in the directory.

Our analysts manually review official business licenses to confirm that the company’s registered business scope includes manufacturing activities. Production capabilities and company data are cross checked against government registries and industry databases. When applicable, ASME and ISO certificates are validated against official issuing body databases.

Export records can also be analyzed to confirm the manufacturer has real international project experience. Additionally, factory locations are verified to ensure that the address corresponds to an actual production facility rather than a sales office.

Through this process, HeatEx Direct helps eliminate intermediaries and reduces sourcing risks for buyers.

---

## Platform Functions

The platform provides two core capabilities: a verified manufacturer directory and a standardized RFQ system.

The manufacturer directory allows engineers and procurement teams to browse verified suppliers based on product types, engineering capabilities, certifications, and export markets. Each supplier profile presents structured technical information that helps buyers quickly evaluate whether the manufacturer is suitable for their project requirements.

The RFQ system allows buyers to generate standardized technical specifications through a guided form. Key engineering parameters such as fluid type, temperature range, pressure level, material requirements, and operating conditions can be entered in a structured format.

Once submitted, the RFQ can be distributed to verified manufacturers that match the required capabilities.

---

## Stealth RFQ Mode

To improve privacy protection during the sourcing process, HeatEx Direct also offers a Stealth RFQ mode.

In this mode, buyers can submit technical specifications without immediately revealing their identity or contact information. This helps prevent unsolicited sales emails and reduces the pressure from suppliers while still allowing procurement teams to compare technical proposals and quotations objectively.

Buyers can choose to reveal their contact information only when they are ready to move forward with specific suppliers.

---

## Value for Manufacturers

HeatEx Direct also provides value to manufacturers.

Verified manufacturers gain access to global procurement teams who are actively searching for industrial heat exchanger suppliers. Instead of competing on large general marketplaces with thousands of listings, manufacturers can present their capabilities in a focused environment designed specifically for engineering buyers.

Manufacturers can maintain detailed company profiles that include technical capabilities, certifications, production capacity, and export experience. The platform also allows manufacturers to publish technical articles, case studies, and maintenance guides to demonstrate engineering expertise.

These technical publications help manufacturers build credibility with international procurement teams and position themselves as trusted industry experts.

---

## Business Model

HeatEx Direct is free for buyers.

Procurement teams can browse verified manufacturer profiles, access technical information, and submit RFQ requests without any platform fees.

The platform generates revenue primarily through referral partnerships with manufacturers. When successful business connections are created, manufacturers may provide a reasonable referral fee. Additional revenue streams may include premium supplier profiles and sponsored technical content.

This model ensures that the platform remains accessible and transparent for buyers while creating value driven opportunities for manufacturers.

---

## Vision

The long term vision of HeatEx Direct is to become the most trusted global gateway for sourcing industrial heat exchangers from China.

By combining manual verification, structured supplier data, and engineering knowledge sharing, the platform aims to create a more reliable and efficient international sourcing ecosystem for both buyers and manufacturers.

# 中文版本

## 项目简介

HeatEx Direct 是一个独立的工业采购平台，旨在直接连接全球采购方与经过验证的中国换热器制造企业。平台专注于换热器行业，包括板式换热器、管壳式换热器、空气冷却器以及其他工业换热设备。

我们的目标是在提升透明度和信任度的同时，简化国际工业设备采购流程。HeatEx Direct 并不是传统意义上的中间商平台，而是一个通过验证机制帮助工程师和采购团队更高效识别可靠制造商的技术型平台。

---

## 行业挑战

在全球采购工业换热设备时，采购方往往会面临许多困难。

大多数在线目录平台收录了成千上万的供应商，但其中真正经过验证的企业非常少。许多企业实际上是贸易公司，而不是实际制造设备的工厂，这使得采购方很难判断供应商的真实能力。

此外，很多供应商网站提供的技术信息并不完整或已经过时。采购团队通常需要花费数周时间进行邮件沟通、证书核验以及企业背景调查，才能建立一个可信的供应商名单。

这种信息不透明的问题在工程类行业尤为严重，因为设备可靠性、合规性以及技术能力都是采购决策中非常关键的因素。

---

## 我们的解决思路

HeatEx Direct 正是为了解决这一信任问题而创建。

与建立一个包含数千家企业的庞大目录不同，我们更专注于筛选出少量真正具备工程能力的制造企业。所有在平台展示的企业都会经过一套结构化的验证流程。

平台分析人员会人工检查企业的营业执照，以确认其经营范围确实包含制造活动。企业的生产能力与公司信息会与政府登记记录以及行业数据库进行交叉验证。如果企业声称拥有 ASME 或 ISO 等认证，我们会通过认证机构数据库进行核验。

在可能的情况下，我们还会通过出口记录分析企业是否具有真实的国际项目经验。同时，平台也会验证工厂地址，以确认该地址确实对应生产设施，而不是销售办公室。

通过这一系列验证流程，HeatEx Direct 能够有效减少中间商，并降低工业采购中的风险。

---

## 平台功能

HeatEx Direct 平台主要包含两个核心功能：制造商目录和标准化询价系统。

制造商目录允许工程师和采购团队根据产品类型、技术能力、认证情况以及出口市场来筛选供应商。每个企业资料页面都会以结构化方式展示关键技术信息，帮助采购方快速判断该制造商是否适合其项目需求。

平台同时提供标准化 RFQ 询价系统。采购方可以通过表单填写关键工程参数，例如流体类型、温度范围、压力等级、材料要求以及运行条件等信息，从而快速生成完整的技术需求。

提交之后，系统会将询价信息发送给符合技术能力要求的已验证制造商。

---

## 匿名 RFQ 模式

为了进一步保护采购方隐私，HeatEx Direct 还提供匿名询价模式。

在该模式下，采购方可以提交技术需求，而无需立即公开身份或联系方式。这可以有效避免大量销售邮件的骚扰，同时仍然允许采购团队对不同供应商的技术方案和报价进行比较。

只有在采购方准备进一步沟通时，才需要公开联系方式。

---

## 对制造商的价值

HeatEx Direct 同样为制造企业创造价值。

通过平台，经过验证的制造商可以接触到正在寻找换热设备供应商的全球采购团队。与大型综合平台相比，HeatEx Direct 提供一个更加聚焦工程采购的环境，使制造商能够更清晰地展示自身能力。

企业可以建立详细的公司资料页面，包括技术能力、认证证书、生产规模以及出口经验。同时，平台还支持技术文章发布，制造商可以分享工程案例、维护指南以及技术分析。

这些内容不仅可以帮助企业建立技术权威，也能够让全球工程师更容易了解企业的专业能力。

---

## 商业模式

HeatEx Direct 对采购方完全免费。

采购团队可以自由浏览已验证供应商信息、查看企业资料并提交询价需求，而无需支付平台费用。

平台的收入主要来自制造商合作推荐费用。当平台促成真实的商业合作时，制造商可能向平台支付合理的推荐服务费。此外，平台未来还可能提供高级企业展示以及技术内容推广等服务。

这种模式能够保证采购方始终可以免费使用平台，同时为制造商提供有价值的商业机会。

## 项目愿景

HeatEx Direct 的长期愿景是成为全球最值得信赖的中国换热器采购门户。

通过人工验证、供应商数据透明化以及工程知识共享，平台希望建立一个更加高效、可靠的国际工业采购生态系统，让全球采购方与中国制造之间的合作更加顺畅。
