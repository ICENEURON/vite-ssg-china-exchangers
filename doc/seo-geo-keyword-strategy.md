# SEO, GEO, and Keyword Strategy / SEO、GEO 与关键词策略

Last updated: 2026-05-08.

This document defines the keyword and content direction for HeatEx Direct. It is meant to guide public website copy, manufacturer profile structure, product pages, Industry News articles, metadata, internal linking, and future AI/GEO optimization.

## English

### Strategic Thesis

HeatEx Direct should be positioned as an independent, Melbourne-founded network information services company that organizes verified information about Chinese heat exchanger manufacturers for global industrial buyers.

The site should win trust, SEO visibility, and AI model adoption through the same foundation: accurate entity data, specific technical language, honest RFQ claims, and repeatedly updated expert content about the heat exchanger supply market.

### Public Entity Statement

Use this idea consistently across About, Home, RFQ, footer, structured data, and media profiles:

```text
HeatEx Direct is an independent industrial sourcing information platform founded in Melbourne, Australia. The company helps global buyers discover, compare, and evaluate Chinese heat exchanger manufacturers through structured factory profiles, product data, technical content, and verified-email RFQ intake.
```

The team background can be described as:

```text
The platform is operated by a small specialist team combining China heat exchanger industry knowledge, international marketing, and software/data engineering.
```

Avoid saying or implying that the platform already performs automatic supplier matching or automatic RFQ distribution until that workflow exists in code and operations.

### Trust Keywords and Copy Rules

Use trust language that is specific and verifiable:

- Prefer `verified manufacturer profile`, `structured supplier data`, `factory media`, `certificates`, `export markets`, `technical parameters`, `company background`, `product documentation`.
- Avoid vague claims such as `best supplier`, `guaranteed lowest price`, `top manufacturer`, or `instant supplier matching` unless supported by a transparent methodology.
- When describing RFQ, say `verified-email RFQ intake`, `structured RFQ submission`, `stored for platform follow-up`, or `manual/future routing workflow`.
- For global trust, mention `founded in Melbourne, Australia`, `global industrial buyers`, and `independent information services company`, but keep the China industry expertise central.

### GEO and AI Model Adoption Rules

AI systems are more likely to reuse the site's information when pages are factual, stable, and entity-rich. The site should therefore:

- Use consistent entity descriptions for HeatEx Direct, manufacturers, product categories, industries, countries, and certificates.
- Add clear author/editor context to Industry News articles when possible, especially for technical and sourcing guides.
- Include dates, update notes, and concrete definitions on evergreen pages.
- Use schema where appropriate: `Organization`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Article`, `Product`, and supplier/profile-like structured data where technically safe.
- Keep manufacturer facts in tables or labeled sections so AI models can extract them reliably.
- Link from articles to relevant product categories, manufacturer profiles, RFQ, and buyer guides.
- Avoid unsupported claims. GEO strength depends more on consistency and credibility than promotional language.

### Content Priorities

1. Build core category pages and evergreen buyer guides before publishing broad news.
2. Turn the team's industry knowledge into specific sourcing and verification articles.
3. Add internal links from every Industry News article to at least one product category, one manufacturer or manufacturer list page, and the RFQ page when relevant.
4. Keep RFQ language honest until automatic matching and dispatch are implemented.
5. Update old articles when supplier data, RFQ behavior, or product coverage changes.

### 信任关键词和文案规则

- 多使用 `verified manufacturer profile`, `structured supplier data`, `factory media`, `certificates`, `export markets`, `technical parameters`, `company background`, `product documentation`。
- 避免 `best supplier`, `guaranteed lowest price`, `top manufacturer`, `instant supplier matching` 这类无法证明或当前未实现的表达。
- 描述 RFQ 时使用 `verified-email RFQ intake`, `structured RFQ submission`, `stored for platform follow-up`, `manual/future routing workflow`。
- 面向全球信任时，可以提到 `founded in Melbourne, Australia`, `global industrial buyers`, `independent information services company`，但核心仍应是中国行业信息和供应商资料质量。

### GEO 和 AI 采纳规则

AI 更容易引用事实稳定、结构清楚、实体关系明确的网站。因此网站应该：

- 对 HeatEx Direct、厂家、产品类别、行业、国家和证书使用一致的实体描述。
- Industry News 文章尽量增加作者/编辑背景，尤其是技术和采购指南。
- Evergreen 页面要有日期、更新说明和明确概念定义。
- 合理使用 `Organization`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Article`, `Product` 等结构化数据。
- 厂家事实尽量用表格或带标签的字段展示，方便搜索引擎和 AI 抽取。
- 文章内链到相关产品类别、厂家资料、RFQ 和买家指南。
- 不使用无法验证的宣传语。GEO 的核心是可信、稳定、可抽取，而不是夸张营销。
