On April 28, 10 removable plate heat exchangers manufactured by Lanzhou Lanshi Heat Exchange Equipment Co., Ltd. for the "Torch Project water-cooling system plate heat exchanger" project successfully passed factory acceptance and were officially completed and shipped.

The delivered heat exchange equipment will mainly support the national nuclear-fusion energy development priority known as the "Torch Project" and serves as core heat exchange equipment for maintaining reliable operation of the project's water-cooling system. In response to the high safety and reliability requirements of the nuclear-fusion device, as well as complex operating conditions involving frequent annual starts and stops and frequent variable-load operation, Lanzhou Lanshi Heat Exchange Equipment Co., Ltd. has, since project launch, strictly benchmarked nuclear-power design and manufacturing specifications, established a full-process quality control mechanism, implemented special deep degreasing during production, and tightly controlled key technical parameters such as water-quality resistivity. While achieving efficient heat transfer, the company also optimized energy-consumption indicators, fully meeting the strict technical and operating requirements of advanced high-end scientific research equipment.

The successful delivery of this equipment is another important practice and technical breakthrough for Lanzhou Lanshi Heat Exchange Equipment Co., Ltd. in the frontier nuclear-fusion energy field. Supported by a deep foundation in heat exchange equipment R&D and manufacturing and a mature nuclear-power quality assurance management system, the company will continue to focus on high-end heat exchange equipment R&D and manufacturing, concentrate on breakthroughs in key core technologies, and contribute Lanshi's strength to the high-quality development of China's high-end energy equipment.
