Recently, 36 large plate heat exchangers manufactured by Lanzhou Lanshi Heat Exchange Equipment Co., Ltd. completed production and factory acceptance and will soon be delivered to the site of a green demonstration project for bioethanol and protein co-production.

Facing challenges such as complex process media, Lanzhou Lanshi Heat Exchange Equipment Co., Ltd. focused precisely during the product R&D stage, using advanced finite element analysis and pressure-bearing calculation technology to closely match the project's core process requirements and achieve accurate, optimized product design. In production, the company relied on intelligent production lines and lean manufacturing processes, together with a closed-loop quality control system for the full process, to finely control every procedure. The final products deliver core advantages including high heat transfer efficiency, strong corrosion resistance, and stable long-term operation, fully meeting the complex operating requirements of the biological fermentation process.

This equipment delivery not only demonstrates the strong technical capability of Lanzhou Lanshi Heat Exchange Equipment Co., Ltd. in large plate heat exchange equipment, but also serves as a vivid example of domestic equipment manufacturing empowering a national strategic emerging industry. Lanzhou Lanshi Heat Exchange Equipment Co., Ltd. will continue to study and implement the spirit of the Fourth Plenary Session of the 20th CPC Central Committee, use technological innovation as the core driver, deepen R&D and manufacturing of efficient energy-saving plate heat exchange equipment, and provide more solid and reliable equipment support for the green transformation and sustainable development of the energy and chemical industry.
