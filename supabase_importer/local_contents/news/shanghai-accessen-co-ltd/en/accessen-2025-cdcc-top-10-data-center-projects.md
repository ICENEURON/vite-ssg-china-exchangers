The 13th China Data Center Standards Summit (CDCC SUMMIT 2025) grandly opened at the Beijing National Convention Center under the theme "Redefining the AI Foundation." Shanghai Accessen, as a provider of data center cooling system solutions, participated in the exhibition showcasing its data center products and low-carbon heat exchange solutions. These included prefabricated integrated cooling stations, liquid cooling hydraulic modules, coolant distribution units (CDUs), and new liquid cooling products (Manifold). Data center cooling systems, being fundamental to safe operation, were a key focus among the attending experts.

In this era of explosive growth in AI computing power, where traditional IDCs are rapidly transitioning to AIDCs, the heat dissipation capacity required for higher cabinet power densities has made cooling systems a core infrastructure element in data centers. Accessen is redefining data center thermal management paths through liquid cooling technology. By integrating prefabrication and intelligent features, it addresses the pain points of cooling efficiency and energy consumption control in high-density computing scenarios. Its philosophy of "standardized design + modular expansion" aligns closely with the industry's low-carbon transformation goals, driving the development of data centers towards greater specialization, integration, convergence, sustainability, collaboration, and security.

Leveraging nearly two decades of product technology and application experience in the heat exchange field, Accessen's prefabricated, low-carbon data center heat exchange solutions offer a product series encompassing both water-cooling and liquid-cooling. The core portfolio includes prefabricated integrated cooling stations, AHRI-certified high-efficiency plate heat exchangers, liquid cooling hydraulic modules, and coolant distribution units. These solutions deeply articulate the technical advantages in terms of energy efficiency improvement, rapid deployment, safe operation and maintenance, and green sustainability, empowering data center clients to break from convention, foster continuous innovation, and achieve sustainable data center development.

<img src="/static/content_posts_image/accessen-2025-cdcc-top-10-data-center-projects/image-03.jpg" alt="Accessen low-carbon data center heat exchange solutions" width="640" />

Achieving rapid, high-quality delivery of efficient data centers has become a core competitive differentiator recognized by the industry. Accessen's leading prefabricated integrated cooling station solutions cover both indoor and outdoor application scenarios. The AIS prefabricated integrated cooling station is a product that structurally optimizes and highly integrates key components of the refrigeration system (including chillers, plate heat exchangers, pumps, cooling towers, valves, intelligent control systems, and auxiliary components). By integrating high-efficiency plate heat exchangers and intelligent control systems, and adopting a factory pre-installed, pre-commissioned model, it operates immediately upon connection to water and power on-site. This reduces project deployment cycles by 50% compared to traditional installation methods. Optimized internal piping and design within the station lower energy consumption by over 20%, meeting the rapid expansion needs of both new and retrofit data center projects.

<img src="/static/content_posts_image/accessen-2025-cdcc-top-10-data-center-projects/image-04.jpg" alt="Accessen AIS prefabricated integrated cooling station solution" width="640" />

Accessen's ACDU series coolant distribution units incorporate modular design and standardized architecture, significantly reducing data center cooling energy consumption and enabling breakthrough optimization of PUE. They support the heat dissipation demands of high-density AI computing deployments, enabling precise and intelligent allocation of cooling capacity to meet the surging cooling needs of AI data centers. Customized liquid cooling system solutions are also available based on client requirements.

<img src="/static/content_posts_image/accessen-2025-cdcc-top-10-data-center-projects/image-05.jpg" alt="Accessen ACDU coolant distribution units" width="640" />

Continuously iterating and upgrading its prefabricated products and solutions, Accessen provides clients with green, low-carbon support characterized by "rapid deployment, energy efficiency optimization, and intelligent regulation." Its solutions have been successfully applied in dozens of projects, including those for China Mobile, China Unicom, Gangcheng Yunlian, GLP Changshu, BDC and GDS. With representative offices established in Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Hong Kong, and other locations, Accessen provides support for data center projects in Southeast Asia and has successfully implemented multiple overseas projects in countries like the Philippines, Thailand, and Malaysia.

The summit also announced a rigorously selected list of 10 outstanding project cases and 17 advanced application technologies. These stand as shining beacons, guiding the development of the data center industry and serving as a "value compass" within the field.

The "2025 Top 10 Outstanding Data Center Projects" award serves as a benchmark demonstration window for data center full-lifecycle practices. It focuses on the safety, reliability, green energy efficiency, and sustainable development capabilities of implemented projects, providing the industry with replicable construction and operation models.
