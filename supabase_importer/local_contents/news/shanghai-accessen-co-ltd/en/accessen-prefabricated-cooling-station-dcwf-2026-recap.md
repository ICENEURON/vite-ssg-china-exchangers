From May 6 to 7, 2026, the highly anticipated Data Centre World Frankfurt 2026 (DCWF) took place at Hall 8, Messe Frankfurt. As a key part of Tech Show Frankfurt, the event brought together nearly 400 global exhibitors and over 12,000 industry professionals. Shanghai Accessen Co., Ltd. (Accessen) made its independent debut at this prestigious event (Booth M155), showcasing its innovative, on-demand prefabricated integrated cooling station solutions. Tailored for the high-density thermal challenges of the AI era, the solutions attracted significant attention and visitor traffic, marking a highly successful exhibition and a major boost in brand influence.

Aligned with the event's core theme, **"Net Zero Data Centres: AI-Ready, Carbon-Smart, Always-On"**, Accessen presented its flagship AIS (Accessen Integrated System) prefabricated cooling stations and comprehensive heat exchange portfolio through an intuitive dynamic display. By integrating chillers, high-efficiency plate heat exchangers, pumps, cooling towers, valves, piping, and intelligent controls into a single, factory-assembled, and pre-tested module, the solution directly addresses the lengthy construction cycles and complex on-site installation challenges faced by the global market. Compared to traditional methods, this modular delivery approach reduces project deployment cycles significantly and enables outdoor plug-and-play operation, perfectly aligning with Europe's urgent demand for rapid and sustainable deployment. Regarding energy efficiency, a key industry focus, Accessen highlighted its landmark Gangcheng Intelligent Manufacturing Data Park project (165 MW IT capacity), which has achieved an industry-leading average PUE of less than 1.25. Its innovative dual-mode design--compatible with both liquid and air-cooled architectures--sparked enthusiastic discussions and strong interest among attendees.

With over two decades of expertise in heat exchange and more than 130 product patents, Accessen has delivered over 20,000 customized thermal solutions worldwide. Its prefabricated cooling stations have been deployed across dozens of major data center projects, serving industry leaders such as China Mobile, ByteDance, Huawei, Tencent, and GLP. Leveraging the significant international influence of DCWF Frankfurt 2026, Accessen not only strengthened direct connections with leading European and global EPC contractors and data center operators--resulting in highly valuable project cooperation intentions--but also further asserted its position as not just a heat exchange specialist from China, but a globally certified provider of highly customized and cost-effective thermal solutions.
