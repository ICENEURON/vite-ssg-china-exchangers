World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade data shows that China exported USD 2,112,449.79 thousand of `HS 841950` non-domestic heat exchange units in 2024, with a recorded quantity of 11,283,600 items. The top five destinations were the United States, Russia, Mexico, Malaysia, and Indonesia.

The UNSD statistical wording for `HS 841950` is **non-domestic heat exchange units**. Data sources: [WITS China HS 841950 exports 2024](https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/CHN/year/2024/tradeflow/Exports/partner/ALL/product/841950), [WITS global HS 841950 exporters 2024](https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/ALL/year/2024/tradeflow/Exports/partner/WLD/product/841950), and [UNSD HS 841950](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/32/841950).

## Data Summary

In 2024, China's `HS 841950` export destinations covered North America, Eurasia, and Southeast Asia. The United States, Russia, Mexico, Malaysia, and Indonesia were the top five destinations, accounting for about 40.59% of China's export value under this code.

Global exporter data shows the European Union, China, the United States, Germany, and Italy in the top five. China recorded USD 2,112,449.79 thousand, below the European Union total and above the individual totals for the United States, Germany, and Italy.
