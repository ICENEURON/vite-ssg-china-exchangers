Lanzhou Lanshi Heat Exchange Equipment Co., Ltd. is a wholly owned subsidiary of Lanshi Heavy Equipment focused on plate heat exchanger research, design, manufacturing, and service. The company continues to build its position around high-end manufacturing, standards participation, and advanced energy applications.

## Manufacturing and Qualification Base

Lanshi Heat Exchange operates from the Lanshi high-end equipment industrial park in Lanzhou New Area, with more than 530,000 square meters of standardized production area and nearly 200 added production equipment sets after relocation. The manufacturing platform also includes a 30,000-ton multi-cylinder thin-plate forming hydraulic press developed by Lanshi.

The qualification base covers ASME, European PED, AHRI, pressure vessel, nuclear quality assurance, plate heat exchanger safety registration, and intellectual property system credentials. The company has also participated in standards work, including nuclear-grade plate heat exchanger design and manufacturing specifications and standards for plate heat exchangers and heat exchange units.

## Technology Milestones

The company's technical history connects to the development of China's first plate heat exchanger in 1965. Lanshi Heat Exchange has developed more than 100 heat exchanger product series and holds more than 60 patents.

The update also points to several high-end application areas: nuclear-grade plate heat exchangers, welded plate heat exchangers, printed circuit heat exchangers, supercritical carbon dioxide power generation recuperators, liquid air energy storage, and large plate-and-shell equipment.

## Industry Signal

For buyers, the company profile is useful because it shows how Lanshi Heat Exchange is positioning itself: not only as a plate heat exchanger maker, but as a supplier for regulated and difficult services where standards, testing, manufacturing capacity, and project references matter together.

The practical takeaway is that qualification evidence and application history should be reviewed alongside price and thermal performance. In nuclear, energy storage, sCO2, and large industrial heat exchange duties, manufacturing control and inspection documentation often become part of the technical value.
