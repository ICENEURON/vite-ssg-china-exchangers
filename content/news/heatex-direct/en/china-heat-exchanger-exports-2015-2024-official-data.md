World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade data shows that China exported USD 2,112,449.79 thousand of `HS 841950` non-domestic heat exchange units in 2024, with a recorded quantity of 11,283,600 items. In 2015, the corresponding export value was USD 740,388.00 thousand and the quantity was 1,441,410 items.

The UNSD product wording for `HS 841950` is **non-domestic heat exchange units**. Classification source: [UNSD HS 841950](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Detail/EN/32/841950). Annual trade data source: [World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade China HS 841950 exports](https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/CHN/year/2024/tradeflow/Exports/partner/ALL/product/841950).

## Data Summary

From 2015 to 2024, China's `HS 841950` export value rose from USD 740.39 million to USD 2.11 billion. Annual export value remained above USD 1.6 billion from 2022 onward and exceeded USD 2.0 billion in both 2023 and 2024.

The quantity series shows comparable endpoints rising from 1,441,410 items in 2015 to 11,283,600 items in 2024. The 2024 quantity was about 10.76% higher than 2023.
