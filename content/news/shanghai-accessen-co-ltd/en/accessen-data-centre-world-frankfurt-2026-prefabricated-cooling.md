Accessen, a leading provider of prefabricated integrated cooling station solutions for data centers, is pleased to announce its participation in Data Centre World Frankfurt 2026 (DCWF), taking place from May 6-7, 2026, at Messe Frankfurt, Hall 8, Germany. Accessen will be exhibiting at Booth M155, where the company will present its latest innovations in modular, prefabricated thermal management solutions designed for next-generation data centers.

As part of Tech Show Frankfurt--the most important technology event in the DACH region--Data Centre World Frankfurt 2026 is expected to attract nearly 400 global exhibitors and over 12,000 industry professionals. The event brings together the region's data center community for two days of innovation, insight, and networking. With its 2026 conference programme focusing on "Net Zero Data Centres: AI-Ready, Carbon-Smart, Always-On," the event provides an ideal platform for Accessen to demonstrate how its prefabricated cooling solutions address the industry's most pressing challenges--balancing performance with sustainability.

The European modular data center market is projected to reach USD 18.60 billion by 2030, growing at a CAGR of 14.6%, driven by stringent EU energy-efficiency regulations and accelerating demand for faster deployment models. Within this landscape, Germany stands as a key market, with the country's data center cooling sector expected to grow from USD 1.15 billion in 2025 to USD 7.16 billion by 2035 at a CAGR of 20.4%. This rapid growth underscores the urgent need for scalable, energy-efficient cooling solutions that can keep pace with AI-driven infrastructure demands.

Accessen is uniquely positioned to meet this demand. With nearly two decades of expertise in heat exchange technology, the company has successfully delivered over 20,000 customized thermal management solutions worldwide, earning the trust of industry leaders including China Mobile, China Unicom, China Telecom, ByteDance, Huawei, Tencent, and GLP. Its prefabricated cooling solutions have been deployed across dozens of major data center projects, providing reliable thermal management for hyperscale, colocation, and AI computing facilities.

## Accelerating Deployment and Enhancing Sustainability

Accessen's prefabricated integrated cooling stations represent a fundamental shift in how data center cooling infrastructure is delivered. By integrating chillers, plate heat exchangers, pumps, cooling towers, valves, and intelligent control systems into a single factory-assembled and pre-tested module, Accessen reduces project deployment cycles by up to 50% compared to traditional on-site installation methods. The company's AIS (Accessen Integrated System) prefabricated integrated cooling station is designed for outdoor deployment, eliminating the need for a separate station house and enabling immediate operation upon connection to water and power on-site.

Beyond speed, Accessen's solutions deliver measurable sustainability benefits. Optimized internal piping and system design reduce energy consumption by over 20%. In landmark projects such as the Gangcheng Intelligent Manufacturing Data Park--a 90-billion-yuan, 165 MW IT capacity facility in China--Accessen's prefabricated cooling station supports an industry-leading average PUE of less than 1.25, well below the 1.55 industry baseline. The company's innovative "dual-mode" design enables seamless compatibility with both air-cooled and liquid-cooled architectures, supporting the high-density thermal requirements of AI workloads while maintaining flexibility for mixed cooling environments.

At DCWF 2026, Accessen will showcase its comprehensive portfolio, including:

- **AIS Prefabricated Integrated Cooling Stations:** Outdoor modular systems that arrive fully assembled and tested, requiring only on-site utility connections for immediate operation
- **Liquid Cooling Hydraulic Modules and ACDU Coolant Distribution Units:** Standardized, modular liquid cooling infrastructure designed to meet the surging cooling demands of high-density AI deployments
- **AHRI-Certified High-Efficiency Plate Heat Exchangers:** Core components that deliver industry-leading thermal performance and reliability

These solutions address the full spectrum of data center cooling needs--from traditional chilled water systems to advanced liquid cooling for AI workloads--empowering operators to achieve faster time-to-market, lower operational costs, and enhanced sustainability.
