Siping Juyuan Hanyang Plate Heat Exchanger Co., Ltd. participated in the 136th China Import and Export Fair in Guangzhou. Through product displays, on-site meetings, and buyer discussions, the company presented its heat exchange equipment, high-efficiency product portfolio, and service capabilities to international purchasers.

## Heat Exchange Products at the 136th Canton Fair

The 136th Canton Fair covered multiple themes, including advanced manufacturing, quality home products, and better living, and attracted overseas buyers from many countries and regions. Siping Juyuan Hanyang Plate Heat Exchanger Co., Ltd. used the exhibition to highlight heat exchange products, digitalization, green development, and high-efficiency equipment.

## International Buyer Engagement

During the fair, the company communicated with customers from Russia, Dubai, India, South Korea, Thailand, Vietnam, Pakistan, Uzbekistan, Iraq, and other markets. Some buyers focused on residential heating equipment, while others discussed marine, industrial, and engineering applications, including product quality, delivery capability, and future cooperation.

## From China Manufacturing to China Intelligent Manufacturing

The Canton Fair helped Siping Juyuan Hanyang Plate Heat Exchanger Co., Ltd. strengthen its connection with the international market. The company said it will continue to compete globally through product quality, energy-efficiency advantages, and service capability.
