Recently, SHPHE completed production and shipment of wide-gap welded plate heat exchangers for a leading Australian mining enterprise.

As a global powerhouse in the mining and aluminum sector, the client enforces stringent standards for equipment performance, quality specifications and operational stability.

As SHPHE’s flagship product, the Wide-Gap WGPHE boasts excellent heat transfer efficiency, robust structural design and long-term operational reliability. It has been operating efficiently at multiple project sites, fully meeting operational requirements and earning sustained recognition, customer trust and repeated orders worldwide.

Manufactured in strict compliance with ASME standards, this batch of equipment has passed inspection by authorized inspectors and obtained U-stamp certification. The entire manufacturing process was subject to on-site supervision by third-party inspection agency APAVE.

With years of profound expertise in heat exchange technology, SHPHE continues to enhance its global service capabilities, delivering safe, efficient and premium heat exchange solutions for customers at home and abroad.
